## Implemented boundary

- `MonitoringCommitProofV1` and `MonitoringWorkspace.load_committed_run()`
  bind watchlist, event batch, run, state and the final state pointer. Missing,
  corrupt, orphaned or identity-mismatched artifacts fail closed.
- `monitoring_execution/contracts.py` defines versioned capability, job,
  attempt and atomic-pointer contracts. Job identity is derived from source
  run, request, policy and committed `as_of`; lifecycle records are terminal
  only, so no false `RUNNING` state survives a crash.
- `monitoring_execution/store.py` keeps job attempts and normalized input,
  analysis, trace, research session/report and surface artifacts separate from
  `MonitoringWorkspace`. Immutable writes, canonical bytes, SHA-256 checks,
  atomic latest-pointer replacement, pointer repair and conflicting-byte
  rejection are covered.
- `monitoring_execution/executor.py` keeps preparation network permission,
  model/runtime availability and explicit accepted adjustments independent.
  It implements all four `ImpactClass` results, fresh PIT-bound preparation,
  optional exact prior Business Quality reuse, scripted FULL research,
  deterministic analysis/trace and validated `ResearchSurfaceSnapshotV1`.
- The executor drops implicit prior adjustment metadata and only calls the
  existing effective-input materialization boundary for caller-supplied
  adjustments. Analyst proposals remain proposals.
- Checked-in Draft 2020-12 schemas cover the new commit proof, capability,
  job, attempt and pointer contracts.
